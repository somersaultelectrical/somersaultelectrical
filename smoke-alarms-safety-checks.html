Smoke Alarm Installation,
Testing & Replacement

Working smoke alarms save lives — and in NSW they're a legal requirement. We install, test and replace smoke alarms for homeowners and landlords across Roseville, Chatswood, Willoughby and Sydney's North Shore, Northern Beaches and CBD.

Smoke alarms are easy to forget until you need them. We make sure yours are the right type, correctly placed, and actually working. Here's how we help:

Smoke Alarms

Installation & Replacement

Hardwired, mains-powered photoelectric smoke alarms installed, and old units replaced. Smoke alarms expire after about 10 years — if yours are older, it's time for new ones.

Testing & Maintenance

We test your alarms, replace backup batteries and make sure everything is working as it should — so you're covered when it counts.

Interconnected Smoke Alarms

Upgrade to interconnected alarms so that when one detects smoke, they all sound — giving everyone in the home earlier warning, especially in bedrooms.

Compliance

Landlord & Rental Compliance

Renting out a property? NSW law requires working smoke alarms. We install, test and check your alarms so your rental meets its obligations.
