Indoors or out, decorative or functional — we install, upgrade and repair lighting with durability and safety in mind. Here's what we do:

Indoor Lighting

Downlight Installation

Quality LED downlights for a clean, modern look, with options and layouts to suit any room and budget.

Pendant & Feature Lights

Found the perfect pendant or chandelier? We'll safely install statement and feature fittings that lift the whole room.

LED Lighting Upgrades

Switch old halogens to LED — around 75% less energy, far longer life, and lower running costs. Hassle-free supply and install.

Switches & Control

Light Switches & Dimmers

Install, replace and repair switches, two-way switches and LED-compatible dimmers for the right atmosphere without flicker.

Smart Switches & Home Automation

Control your lights and power from your phone or by voice. We install smart switches and relays — Wi-Fi and Zigbee systems including Shelly, Sonoff and Pixie — so you can automate lighting and set schedules, no full rewire needed.

Outdoor Lighting

Outdoor & Entertaining Lighting

Alfresco, patio and entryway lighting to make your outdoor spaces usable and welcoming after dark.

Garden & Landscape Lighting

Up-lighting for trees and features, soft ambient beds and path lights to bring your garden to life, day and night.

Driveway & Path Lighting

Bollards, in-ground and motion-sensor LEDs for a great first impression and safer access.

Pool & Security Lighting

Durable pool lighting, plus hardwired sensor and security lights to keep your property safe and well-lit.

Repairs & Seasonal

Light Repairs & Replacements

Flickering, dead or buzzing lights? Downlight stopped working? We find the fault fast and repair or replace fittings, switches and wiring to get everything working safely again.

Christmas & Festive Lighting

Want a standout display without the ladder risk? We install and hang Christmas and festive lighting, set up safe outdoor power and timers, and can take it all down in the new year.
