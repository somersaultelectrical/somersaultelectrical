⚡ FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much does a switchboard upgrade cost in Sydney?

Switchboard upgrade costs vary depending on your property's age, the number of circuits, and whether safety switches or additional capacity are needed. Contact Somersault Electrical on 0448 888 004 for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your property.

Do I need a licensed electrician for switchboard or wiring work in NSW?

Yes. Any electrical installation, repair, or switchboard work in NSW must be carried out by a licensed electrician to meet Australian safety standards. Somersault Electrical is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial work.

What suburbs does Somersault Electrical service?

We service Sydney's Northern Beaches (from Manly to Palm Beach), the Sydney CBD, the Upper North Shore (from Roseville to Wahroonga), and the Lower North Shore (including Mosman, Chatswood, and Willoughby).

Do you offer emergency electrical repairs?

Yes, we offer emergency and urgent electrical repairs for issues like power outages, faults, and safety concerns. Call 0448 888 004 directly for urgent callouts.

Do you work on strata properties and unit blocks?

Yes, we carry out electrical maintenance and repairs for strata buildings, unit blocks, and retail shops across the Lower North Shore, alongside standard residential work.

How quickly can you respond to a call-out?

Response times depend on the day and job type, but we prioritise urgent safety issues and aim for prompt, same-day service where possible. Call 0448 888 004 to check current availability.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, we offer free, competitive quotes for all electrical work. Get in touch via phone or the contact form on this site.
